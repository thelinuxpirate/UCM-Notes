- Sampling Error Solution = Simple Random Sample (SRS) -> with replacement
  (guarantees that everyone in population has equal likelihood of showing up in data)
- Randomization Error | Anytime the treatment or control group are different in
   any measurable way, before, during, or after the experiment in a way other
   than the treatment
  - Double Blind: Eliminates the placebo effect
- Modeling Error / Perceptual Error
  - Omitted Variables | Missing Variables that lead to a misinterpretaiton of data
  - Reverse Causality = Inverse interpretation of a model
  - Spurious Correlations = Correlations != causal relationship

* Lecture 7 | Finishing up Descriptives, Visualizing Data
- (Pearson's) Kurtosis = How concentrated is the data towards the center of in the tails?
  - K > 3  (Leptokurtic)                        = Extremely Concentrated in the center of the data, *peaked* & slope is *steep* (graph)
  - K ~= 3 (Mesokurtic AKA Normal Distribution) = Concentrated in the middle but can spill out between the tails of the graph (Medium *peaked*)
  - K < 3  (Platykurtic)                        = NOT heavily concentrated towards the middle, most of the weight in the tails
