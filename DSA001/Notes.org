* Chapter 2 | Cultivating Your Digital Presence
** Communicating via your Digital Presence
- Digital Presence = Learning strategies to compensate for the mutual
  knowledge problem
- Jenna's career turned digital
- Problem with Hybrid work: You feel like youre working solo instead of with a team
- Good Digital Presence in the workplace = Giving a lot of status updates to your coworkers
- Common Fear via Hybrid work: Over-communication | Take-away: Not an issue

- Ambiguity in communication:
  - Not always flawed, create a sense of curiosity which can be useful
  - Payoff: Make your messages worth the wait or else your later messages will be tuned out
  - Timing: Don't fill in the person too soon, give the message time to strike more curiosity
  - Frequency: Don't do this to the same person more than every 4-6 months

- Meta knowledge = Is Knowledge of ‘Who Knows What’ and ‘Who Knows Whom’
