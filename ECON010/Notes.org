* Lecture 3 | Measurement, Modeling, & Error
- Types of Measurements (Quantitative)
  - Nominal  = Purely categorical | Countable
  - Ordinal  = Numbers (scale) doesnt matter, zero is arbitrary, more vs. less matters, internally consistent
  - Interval = Scale is consistent but zero is arbitrary | Externally consistent | EX: Tempature
  - Ratio    = Scale is consistent but zero has meaning | more vs. less matters | Externally consistent
               When we put 2 ratio vars into a ratio we gain access to new info

*** Purpose of a Model
- Replicating a past result

*** Common Types (causes) of Error
- Measurement Error | Any consistent rounding that occurs
  -> How to minimize error = anonymize data collection
- Sampling Error | When your sample is not consistent with population being studied
  -> Online Polls example of sampling error
* Lecture 4 | Error Types & Strategies to Mitigate Continued...
** Error Solutions
- Sampling Error Solution = Simple Random Sample (SRS) -> with replacement
  (guarantees that everyone in population has equal likelihood of showing up in data)
- Randomization Error | Anytime the treatment or control group are different in
   any measurable way, before, during, or after the experiment in a way other
   than the treatment
  - Double Blind: Eliminates the placebo effect
- Modeling Error / Perceptual Error
  - Omitted Variables | Missing Variables that lead to a misinterpretaiton of data
  - Reverse Causality = Inverse interpretation of a model
  - Spurious Correlations = Correlations != causal relationship
- ^How to solve all of this = Peer-Review
- Random Error | Samll positive & negative variations that cant be eliminated from our variables
  -> collect more data to solve
- Cross-Section | Survey Questions, to a select group/population
  - Same Questions, Different People
- Panel/Lougitudinal Data = Same Questions, Same People
